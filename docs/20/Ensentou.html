How to Use Hot Springs and Public Bathhouses

日本語

Here's how to prepare before bathing and observe proper etiquette in the bath to enjoy a comfortable experience at Japanese public bathhouses.

🚿 Basic Manners Before Bathing

Wash and Cleanse Your Body

Before entering the bathtub, it is essential to wash and rinse your body thoroughly. This practice helps keep the bathwater clean.

🛀 Manners in the Bathtub

Do Not Put Towels in the Water

It is considered unhygienic to put towels in the bathtub. Place your towel on your head or on the edge of the bathtub instead.

Use Quietly

Avoid talking loudly or swimming in the bathtub. Be considerate so that other guests can relax comfortably.

🚫 About Tattoos

Many hot springs and public bathhouses in Japan refuse entry to people with tattoos. Although regulations have been gradually relaxed in recent years, it is important to check in advance.
