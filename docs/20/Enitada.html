Meal Greetings (Itadakimasu / Gochisousama)

日本語版

Explaining the meaning and proper usage of greetings said before and after meals.

🙏 "Itadakimasu" Meaning and Manners

Origin of the Greeting

"Itadakimasu" expresses gratitude for the blessings of life in the meal, including all the people and nature that nurtured it, as well as the lives of the plants and animals that became the ingredients.

Usage

  • Place your hands together and bow slightly, then say this just before starting your meal.
  • It also serves as a greeting to those dining with you, meaning "Excuse me for starting before you."

😌 "Gochisousama deshita" Meaning and Manners

Origin of the Kanji

"馳走 (chisou)" once meant to run around to prepare a meal. Therefore, "gochisousama" contains gratitude towards those who worked hard to prepare the meal.

Usage

  • After finishing your meal, gather your dishes and say this before leaving your seat.
  • It is a word of gratitude directed towards everyone involved in preparing the meal, including family members and staff.
