Correct Use and Etiquette of Chopsticks

日本語版

This section explains the basic way to hold chopsticks beautifully and the "disliked chopstick behaviors" to avoid for an enjoyable dining experience.

🥢 Correct Way to Hold Chopsticks

Using chopsticks beautifully makes mealtime more enriching. The basic principle is to move the two chopsticks independently.

  1. **Bottom chopstick**: Fix it at the base of the ring finger and thumb, and do not move it.
  2. **Top chopstick**: Hold it like a pencil with the index finger, middle finger, and thumb, and move only this chopstick to grasp food.

🚫 Disliked Chopstick Behaviors (Taboo Actions)

The following actions are called "disliked chopstick behaviors" and are considered manners violations that make dining companions and the dining environment uncomfortable.

  • **Stabbing with chopsticks**: Eating by piercing food with chopsticks.
  • **Wavering chopsticks**: Moving chopsticks over dishes while deciding what to eat.
  • **Resting chopsticks**: Placing chopsticks across a bowl like a bridge (considered a sign of pausing or finishing a meal).
  • **Licking chopsticks**: Licking food off the chopsticks.
