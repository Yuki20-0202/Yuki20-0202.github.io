How to Offer Money and Proper Worship Etiquette

日本語版

This section explains the correct worship procedures and how to offer money to show respect to gods and Buddha at shrines and temples.

🪙 Meaning and How to Offer Money

What is Saisen?

Saisen is an offering made to establish a connection with gods or Buddha, or to express daily gratitude and wishes. There is no set amount for the offering.

How to Offer Money

When offering money, do not throw it roughly but place it gently into the offering box. There is a custom that making a sound (e.g., dropping a five-yen coin) is considered auspicious, but basically, it is offered quietly.

🙏 Basic Worship at Shrines

At shrines, the general procedure for worship is as follows.

  1. Offer money.
  2. **Ring the bell** (to summon the gods).
  3. **Two bows** (bow twice).
  4. **Two claps** (clap twice).
  5. **One bow** (bow once more).

Worship at Temples

At temples, the basic practice is to put your hands together and pray quietly. Unlike shrines, clapping hands is not done.
