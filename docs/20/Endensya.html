Etiquette on the Train (Public Transportation Manners)

日本語版

This section covers important points and manners for using public transportation comfortably in Japan.

📵 Etiquette for Mobile Phones and Conversations on the Train

Culture of Silence

On trains, it is common to refrain from making phone calls and to set phones to silent mode. Be mindful of sound leakage from earphones and try to keep the environment as quiet as possible.

💺 Priority Seats and Offering Seats

Priority Seats

Near priority seats, it is important to turn off your mobile phone or set it to silent mode and refrain from making calls. Additionally, it is important to offer your seat to elderly people, pregnant women, and those with disabilities.

Handling Luggage

Do not keep backpacks or large luggage on your back; instead, hold them in front of you or place them on the overhead rack. Especially during crowded times, be careful not to inconvenience other passengers.
