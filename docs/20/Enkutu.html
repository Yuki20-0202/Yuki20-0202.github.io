Proper Manners for Removing and Putting on Shoes

日本語版

The boundary between "inside" and "outside" in Japan. This section explains where you should remove your shoes and how to handle them after removal.

🚪 Places Where You Should Remove Shoes (Distinguishing Inside and Outside)

Main Places

In Japanese culture, shoes are removed at the entrance of a house to distinguish between "inside" and "outside." Generally, shoes are removed in the following places:

  • Typical Japanese houses and apartments
  • Restaurants with Japanese-style rooms or tatami rooms
  • Temples and Shrines (in specific areas)
  • Ryokan and traditional lodging facilities

👟 Proper Etiquette at the Entrance

How to Remove Shoes

When removing shoes, it is polite to turn slightly so as not to turn your back to others. After removing your shoes, step onto the **raised entrance step (agari-kamachi)** and then squat down to neatly arrange your shoes.

How to Place Shoes

After removing your shoes, arrange them neatly with the **toes pointing towards the entrance** (outside) so as not to obstruct the host, and place them to the side.
