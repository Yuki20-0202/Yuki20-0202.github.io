Kyoto Transport & Disaster Information

Welcome to the Kyoto Transport & Disaster Information Site!
This portal provides links to various public transportation operators in Kyoto, disaster hazard maps, and real-time river level information. We also provide guides on how to use local buses and trains. This site is part of a research project by students from Osaka Metropolitan University College of Technology. We appreciate your feedback to help improve our future research. Please note that this site uses AI for translation, so there may be mistranslations. Additionally, some sites may not support English.

Help & FAQ

Q. What can I do on this site?

You can quickly access service information for various railway companies in Kyoto City, as well as river level data and hazard maps published by Kyoto Prefecture and Kyoto City.

Q. How can I check train status?

Please click on the buttons for each railway company in the "Train Status" section. The official top page of each operator will open in a separate window.

Q. I am not sure how to use buses or the subway.

By clicking the "Kyoto City Subway & Bus" button, you can access the official "Aruku Machi Kyoto" portal or the "User Guide." These pages provide detailed information on how to pay fares and how to transfer.

Q. How can I contribute to the research?

We invite you to use the site and share your feedback, such as any inconveniences or suggestions for improvement, through the survey link on the "Tabisuke" TOP page.
