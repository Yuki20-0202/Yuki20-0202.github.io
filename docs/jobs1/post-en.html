Consent Regarding Labor Standards Act

If working hours exceed 8 hours, please review the relevant articles of the Labor Standards Act below and confirm your agreement. (Scroll to read)

Article 32: Working Hours

Working hours shall not exceed 8 hours per day or 40 hours per week in principle.

Article 36: Overtime and Holiday Work

Employers must conclude an agreement (so-called Article 36 Agreement) with a labor union or a representative of the majority of workers to allow overtime or holiday work.

Article 37: Overtime Pay

Specifies premium wages for overtime, holiday, and late-night work.
