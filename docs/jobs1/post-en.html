Consent regarding the Labor Standards Act (sample)

Please review the following excerpts of the Labor Standards Act and agree that this posting is based on them. (You can scroll vertically to view.)

Article 32 (Working Hours)

Working hours shall not exceed 8 hours per day and 40 hours per week, in principle.

Article 36 (Overtime and Holiday Work)

Employers must enter into an agreement with the representative of workers (a "36-agreement") and file it to have workers perform overtime or holiday work.

Article 37 (Premium Wages)

Provides for premium wages for overtime, holiday, and late-night work.
